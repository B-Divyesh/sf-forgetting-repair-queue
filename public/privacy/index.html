Your study history stays on your device.

Repair Queue is a local-first utility. Import analysis, the untouched source export, repair decisions, notes, and backups are processed and stored by your browser. We do not receive them.

What is stored locally

The app uses IndexedDB for the active import and repair plan, Cache Storage for offline app files, and localStorage for an optional purchase license and its cached verification result. You can download your original file and backup, or delete local study data from the workbench.

License verification

If you buy or restore Workbench Plus, your license token is sent to api.sociobot.in only to verify that it is valid for this product. The result is cached for up to 24 hours. Sociobot / Dodo is the merchant of record and handles checkout and refunds under its own transaction records.

No tracking

The app includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, trackers, third-party fonts, or runtime scripts. Server access logs may temporarily contain ordinary request metadata such as IP address and user agent for security and operations.

Your choices

Clear local data in the workbench, remove the site’s stored data through browser settings, or remove a saved license from the unlock section. Downloaded copies remain under your control.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
